## 1. Project Description

Ehda Portal is an online blood donation platform designed to connect people in need of blood with potential blood donors, hospitals, and blood donation centers. It provides a centralized platform where users can create donor profiles, search for potential donors, and send blood requests.

The platform allows donors to provide information such as blood type, contact information, approximate location, and availability status. Blood seekers can search for potential donors based on blood type and location and explore nearby donors and medical facilities through an interactive map. Donors can receive blood requests and choose to accept or decline them.

Ehda Portal also provides notifications about blood requests and enables messaging between donors and blood seekers after a request has been accepted. Donors can maintain records of their previous donations, while administrators can manage users, hospitals, donation centers, and accounts through an administrative dashboard.

The platform aims to make finding potential blood donors more organized, accessible, and efficient. It is intended to support communication and coordination between blood seekers and donors while protecting users' personal information. Medical professionals remain responsible for confirming donor eligibility and blood compatibility.

## 2. Problem Statement

Finding suitable blood donors can be time-consuming, particularly when blood is needed urgently. People may have difficulty identifying potential donors with the required blood type or locating donors in nearby areas. Information about available donors and nearby hospitals or blood donation centers may also be difficult to access in one place.

Ehda Portal aims to address these challenges by providing an online platform where users can search for potential donors based on blood type and location, view relevant medical facilities, submit blood requests, and communicate with donors through an organized process.

By bringing these functions together, the platform seeks to simplify donor discovery and improve coordination between people seeking blood and potential donors.

## 3. Project Objectives

The main objectives of Ehda Portal are:

1. To provide an online platform that connects people in need of blood with potential donors.
2. To allow donors to create and manage profiles containing their blood type, contact information, approximate location, and availability status.
3. To enable blood seekers to search for potential donors by blood type and location.
4. To provide an interactive map displaying approximate donor locations, hospitals, and blood donation centers.
5. To allow blood seekers to submit blood requests to potential donors.
6. To enable donors to accept or decline incoming blood requests.
7. To provide notifications about blood requests and their responses.
8. To enable messaging between donors and blood seekers after a request has been accepted.
9. To allow donors to maintain and review records of their previous donations.
10. To provide an administrative dashboard for managing users, hospitals, donation centers, and accounts.
11. To protect user privacy by restricting access to sensitive information, including donors' exact home addresses.

## 4. Target Users

The main target users of Ehda Portal are described below.

### 4.1 Blood Donors

Blood donors are people willing to donate blood, subject to medical eligibility. They can create and manage profiles, provide their blood type and availability information, receive blood requests, respond to requests, communicate with blood seekers when permitted, and maintain donation records.

### 4.2 Blood Seekers

Blood seekers are people looking for blood for themselves or others. They can search for potential donors by blood type and location, view permitted donor information, submit blood requests, receive responses, and communicate with donors after request acceptance.

### 4.3 Hospitals and Blood Donation Centers

Hospitals and blood donation centers are medical facilities that users can locate through the platform. Their locations and relevant information can be displayed on the interactive map to help users identify nearby facilities and coordinate where donation-related activities may take place.

### 4.4 Administrators

Administrators are responsible for managing the platform's users and supported facility records. They can perform authorized account-management tasks, manage hospital and donation-center information, review reported accounts, and monitor platform activity through the administrative dashboard.

## 5. Main Features

### 5.1 Registration and Login

Users can create accounts and log in to access the platform's authorized features.

### 5.2 Donor Profiles

Donors can create and update their profiles, including their blood type, contact information, approximate location, and availability status.

### 5.3 Blood Donor Search

Blood seekers can search for potential donors by blood type and location. Where supported, search results can be filtered or ordered by distance and donor availability.

### 5.4 Interactive Map

The map displays approximate donor locations and the locations of supported hospitals and blood donation centers. Exact donor home addresses must not be exposed to ordinary users.

### 5.5 Blood Requests

Blood seekers can submit requests to potential donors. Each request has a status, such as pending, accepted, or declined.

### 5.6 Notifications

Donors can receive notifications about incoming blood requests, and relevant users can receive updates about request responses through the platform's notification mechanism.

### 5.7 Messaging

Donors and blood seekers can communicate through the platform after the relevant blood request has been accepted.

### 5.8 Donation History

Donors can record and view information about their previous donations and update records where permitted.

### 5.9 Administrative Dashboard

Administrators can manage users and supported hospital and donation-center records, review reported accounts, and monitor relevant platform activity.

## 6. Project Scope

Ehda Portal focuses on donor discovery, blood request coordination, communication, donation record management, and administrative functions. Its main purpose is to help users find and contact potential donors more efficiently.

The platform does not independently determine medical eligibility, guarantee that a donor is available, or confirm blood compatibility. Such decisions must be made through appropriate medical procedures and qualified healthcare professionals.

The system should also protect personal information by limiting access to sensitive account details and using approximate locations in public-facing donor search and map features.
