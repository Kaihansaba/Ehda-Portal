Find blood donors near you. Save a life.

Ehda lists people in Afghanistan who are willing to give blood, so patients, families and hospitals can find a matching donor nearby. It is free to use. Donors’ exact addresses are never shown.

Ehda (اهدا) means “donation” in Dari.

How it works

  1. Create an account

    Sign up as a donor or as a hospital.

  2. Add your blood type and area

    Donors choose a rough location on a map. Others only see an area of about 1 km.

  3. Search and get in touch

    Search by blood type and distance to see available donors nearby.
