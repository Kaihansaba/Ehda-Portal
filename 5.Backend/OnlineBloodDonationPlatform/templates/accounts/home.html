How it works

Three simple steps between someone who needs blood and someone who can give it.

  1. 1

    Create a free account

    Sign up as a donor or as a hospital. It takes about a minute.

  2. 2

    Share your blood type and area

    Donors add their blood type and an approximate location. Your exact address stays private.

  3. 3

    Connect and donate

    People in need search by blood type and distance, then reach out to available donors nearby.

The 8 blood types

Who can give to whom (red blood cells). A hospital always confirms compatibility with a lab test before a transfusion.
