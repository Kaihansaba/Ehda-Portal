The purpose of this meeting is to review the work planned for Sprint 3, check whether the implemented features meet their acceptance criteria, discuss any problems or unfinished tasks, and collect feedback before planning Sprint 4.

## Expected Outcomes

- Review the actual progress of Sprint 3 backlog items #011–#014.
- Confirm whether blood request notifications, request responses, and messaging work as expected.
