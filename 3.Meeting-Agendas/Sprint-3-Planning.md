## Meeting Purpose

The purpose of the Sprint 3 planning meeting is to establish the sprint goal, select the work the development team plans to complete, discuss how the selected tasks will be implemented, and agree on responsibilities.

Sprint 3 focuses on the blood request process, allowing donors and people seeking blood to respond to requests and communicate more effectively. The planned work includes notifications, accepting or declining requests, displaying request responses, and messaging after a request is accepted.

## Sprint 3 Goal

The goal of Sprint 3 is to improve communication between people seeking blood and potential donors by supporting request notifications, donor responses, and messaging after a request is accepted.

By the end of the sprint, the team aims to demonstrate the request-response workflow, test the related features, and update the project documentation.
