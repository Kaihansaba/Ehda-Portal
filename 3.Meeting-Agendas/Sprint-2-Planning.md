## Meeting Purpose

The purpose of this meeting is to plan the work for Sprint 2, agree on the sprint goal, select the tasks from the product backlog, and decide who will be responsible for each task.

Sprint 2 focuses on helping users find potential blood donors by blood type and location. The team will also work on displaying approximate donor locations to protect their privacy and implementing an interactive map showing donors, hospitals, and healthcare facilities.

## Sprint 2 Goal

The goal of Sprint 2 is to allow users to search for potential blood donors by blood type and location while protecting donor privacy. The team also aims to implement an interactive map to help users view nearby donors and relevant healthcare facilities.
