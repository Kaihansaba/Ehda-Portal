## Expected Outcomes

- Agree on the Sprint 4 goal and the highest-priority remaining tasks.
- Confirm the backlog items selected for Sprint 4.
- Clarify each developer’s responsibilities and any dependencies between features.
- Agree on how the team will test the application, fix bugs, and retest changes.
- Identify the documentation and GitHub repository materials that still need preparation or review.
- Record any blockers, decisions, and follow-up actions.

## Preparation Before the Meeting

- The Product Owner should review the backlog, priorities, and acceptance criteria.
- The Scrum Master should prepare the meeting agenda and help coordinate the discussion.
- Developers should review their current progress and identify unfinished work, technical dependencies, and blockers.
- The team should check the status of the SRS, diagrams, test evidence, source code, database schema, and other required submission materials.
