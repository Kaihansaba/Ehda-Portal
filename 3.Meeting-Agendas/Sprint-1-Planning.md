## Objective

The purpose of this meeting is to review the user stories and product backlog, discuss backlog priorities and acceptance criteria, identify the tasks planned for Sprint 1, clarify initial task assignments, and agree on the sprint's objectives and expected deliverables. The team will also discuss documentation requirements, GitHub repository organization, and the Scrum meeting schedule.
