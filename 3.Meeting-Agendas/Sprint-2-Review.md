## Meeting Purpose

The purpose of the Sprint 2 Review Meeting is to review the work completed during the sprint, demonstrate the available features, check them against the acceptance criteria, and discuss any problems the team encountered.

Sprint 2 focused on improving the donor search process. The planned features included searching for donors by blood type and location, displaying approximate donor locations to protect their privacy, and adding an interactive map to help users find nearby donors, hospitals, and healthcare facilities.

During the meeting, the team will discuss the progress made, demonstrate the implemented features, and identify any unfinished tasks, defects, or blockers that need further attention.

## Completed Work Before Sprint 2

Sprint 1 focused on establishing the foundation of Ehda Portal. The planned work included setting up the project structure, connecting the database, implementing user registration and authentication, and preparing donor profiles with blood type and availability information.

This work was intended to provide the foundation needed for the search and map features in Sprint 2.

## Sprint 2 Goals

The goal of Sprint 2 was to help users find potential blood donors by blood type and location while protecting donor privacy.

The planned outcomes were:

- Search for donors by blood type.
- Search for donors by location or distance.
- Display approximate donor locations without revealing exact home addresses.
- Provide an interactive map showing relevant nearby locations.
- Validate user input and handle errors.
- Test the implemented features and record any defects or blockers.
