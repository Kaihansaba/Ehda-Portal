## Objective

The purpose of this meeting is to introduce the Ehda Portal project to all team members, explain the problem the platform aims to address, discuss its objectives and main features, clarify team members' Scrum roles and responsibilities, and establish a shared understanding of the project scope and expected deliverables.

## Expected Outcomes

By the end of the kickoff meeting, the team is expected to:

- Understand the purpose and scope of Ehda Portal.
- Understand the platform's main features and intended users.
- Know each team member's Scrum role and responsibilities.
- Share a common understanding of the project objectives.
- Be prepared to discuss user stories, the product backlog, and sprint planning in a separate meeting.
